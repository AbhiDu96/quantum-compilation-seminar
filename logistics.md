## Weekly plan

Each week has five sessions across three days, roughly four hours in total. The [schedule](schedule.md) shows which topic runs in which week.

| Day | Session | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday | Instructor presentation of the week's topic | 45 min |
| Tuesday | Open discussion | 45 min |
| Thursday | Discussion session about the paper or topic you want to present | 45 min |
| Friday | Student presentation | 45 min |
| Friday | Discussion and feedback | 45 min |

## Before you join

- Read the [expectations](expectations.md) to see what is asked of participants, including the student presentation.
- A working knowledge of quantum computing fundamentals is recommended. No background in quantum error correction is assumed.
